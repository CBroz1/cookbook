# Cornbread

## Shopping

- Produce (none)
- Dairy
    - butter (.5c, plus more for serving)
    - milk (1.25c)
    - eggs (3)
- Middle
    - cornmeal (1c)
- Staples
    - flour (2c)
    - sugar (1c)
    - baking powder (1.5tbsp)
    - salt (1tsp)
    - oil (.5c)
    - honey (opt)

## Prep

1. Grease 9x13 pan
1. Melt butter (.5c), let cool
1. Dry bowl, whisk
    - flour (2c)
    - cornmeal (1c)
    - sugar (1c)
    - baking powder (1.5tbsp)
    - salt (1tsp)
1. Wet bowl, mix
    - butter (.5c), melted
    - oil (.5c)
    - milk (1.25c)
    - eggs (3)

## Cook

1. Oven to 350
1. Pour wet into dry, mix just until combined
1. Transfer batter to pan
1. Bake until golden and a toothpick comes out clean (35-45m)
1. Cool in pan (15-20m), cut into squares
1. Serve with butter and honey (opt)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 45m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 35m
- Servings: 16 slices
