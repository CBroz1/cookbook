# Pizza Beans

## Shopping

- Produce
    - yellow onion (1)
    - garlic (3)
    - baby spinach (5oz, opt)
    - basil (opt)
- Dairy
    - low-moisture part-skim mozzarella (4oz, 1c)
- Middle
    - beans (30oz, 2 cans): cannellini, Great Northern or chickpeas
    - tomato paste (6-7oz)
- Staples
    - olive oil (.25c)
    - salt (.5tsp)
    - red pepper flakes (.25tsp, opt)

## Prep

1. Drain and rinse beans (30oz, 2 cans)
1. Mash beans (.5c) with a fork, set aside (opt, thickens sauce)
1. Halve and very thinly slice onion (1)
1. Thinly slice garlic (3)
1. Grate mozzarella (4oz, 1c)
1. Measure water (.67c)
1. Tear basil (opt)

## Cook

1. Oven to 450
1. Oven-safe 10in skillet, med-high heat, olive oil (.25c)
    1. Add onion (1), salt (.25tsp), stir often until tender (3-5m)
    1. Add garlic (3), red pepper flakes (.25tsp, opt)
    1. Stir constantly until garlic edges turn golden (30s)
    1. Add tomato paste (6-7oz), scrape up browned bits, cook (30s)
    1. Add spinach (5oz, opt) in halves, stir until wilted (20s each)
    1. Add beans and mashed beans, water (.67c), salt (.25tsp)
    1. Scrape up browned bits, bring to a simmer
    1. Top evenly with mozzarella (4oz, 1c)
1. Bake until cheese is nicely melted (7-9m)
1. Cool in skillet (5m); the handle stays hot
1. Top with basil (opt) and serve warm

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 30m
    - Prep: 12m
    - Cook: 18m
- Servings: 4
