# Roasted Cauliflower Dip

## Shopping

- Produce
    - cauliflower (1 head, 2lb, 6c florets)
    - garlic (1)
    - lemon (1)
    - parsley
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - tahini (.25c)
    - mixed olives (.33c)
    - crackers, for serving
- Staples
    - olive oil (5tbsp)
    - kosher salt (1.5tsp)

## Prep

1. Cauliflower (2lb), core and cut into florets (6c)
1. Mince garlic (1) into a bowl, pour lemon juice (1) over it, let sit while the
    cauliflower roasts - this tames the garlic
1. Mince parsley
1. Set aside
    - tahini (.25c)
    - warm water (.5c)
    - mixed olives (.33c)

## Cook

1. Oven to 400
1. Toss cauliflower with olive oil (2tbsp) and salt (.75tsp)
1. Roast, stirring once, until tender and browned (35m)
1. Food processor
    1. Roasted cauliflower
    1. Olive oil (2tbsp)
    1. Garlic and lemon juice
    1. Tahini (.25c)
    1. Warm water (.5c)
    1. Salt (.75tsp)
    1. Blend until completely smooth, more water if needed
1. Spoon onto a plate, make a well in the middle, drizzle with olive oil (1tbsp)
1. Garnish with olives (.33c) and parsley, serve with crackers

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 45m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 35m
- Servings: 6
