# Smoked Maple Tofu Salad

!!! note
    All quantities are doubled from the source, which serves 2-3. The source lists
    soy sauce and cornstarch once each under the tofu, then splits each between
    dusting the tofu and thickening the glaze; that split is spelled out here.

## Shopping

- Produce
    - carrots (4)
    - red pepper (2)
    - cabbage (2 wedges)
    - cucumber (1 long)
    - daikon (2 small)
    - red chili pepper (1)
    - scallions (2)
    - mint (2 handfuls) or cilantro (2 handfuls)
    - lime juice (4tbsp, 2 limes)
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - firm tofu (400g)
    - peanut oil (4tbsp)
    - liquid smoke (2tsp)
- Staples
    - soy sauce or tamari (8tbsp)
    - maple syrup (4tbsp plus 4tsp)
    - neutral oil (2tbsp)
    - toasted sesame oil (2tsp)
    - cornstarch (4tbsp)
    - rice vinegar (3tbsp)
    - Sriracha (2tsp)

## Prep

!!! note
    Press the tofu first; the rest of the prep fits inside that window.

1. Press tofu (400g): wrap in towels, weigh down, swap towels when wet (20m)
1. Cut tofu into long, thin strips
1. Toss tofu with soy sauce (2tbsp), spread on a plate
1. Dust tofu with cornstarch (2tbsp) in a thin layer
1. Salad bowl
    - carrots (4), shaved into ribbons
    - red pepper (2), thin strips
    - cabbage (2 wedges), sliced fine
    - cucumber (1 long), half-moons
    - daikon (2 small), half-moons
    - red chili pepper (1), sliced thin
    - scallions (2), sliced thin
    - mint or cilantro (2 handfuls)
1. Dressing bowl, whisk
    - neutral oil (2tbsp)
    - lime juice (4tbsp)
    - soy sauce (4tbsp)
    - maple syrup (4tsp)
    - toasted sesame oil (2tsp)
1. Glaze pot, whisk smooth
    - maple syrup (4tbsp)
    - rice vinegar (3tbsp)
    - soy sauce (2tbsp)
    - Sriracha (2tsp)
    - liquid smoke (2tsp)
    - cornstarch (2tbsp)

## Cook

1. Small pot, glaze, medium heat
    1. Whisk to a boil
    1. Boil until thick enough to coat a spoon (1-2m)
    1. Off heat, set aside to cool
1. Small pan, medium-high heat, peanut oil (4tbsp)
    1. Fry tofu until crisp on all sides, turning often (6-8m) (2 batches)
    1. Toss hot tofu in the glaze to coat
1. Stir dressing through the salad
1. Top with glazed tofu and serve

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 60m
    - Prep: 20m
    - Cook: 20m
    - Rest: 20m
- Servings: 4-6
