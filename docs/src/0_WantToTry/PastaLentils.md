# Spaghetti with Lentils and Marinara

## Shopping

- Produce
    - garlic (1)
    - fresh basil (opt)
- Dairy
    - Parmesan (opt)
- Middle
    - marinara sauce (2c)
    - whole-grain pasta (8oz)
    - vegetable broth (2c)
- Staples
    - dry lentils, French green or brown (.5c)
    - bay leaf (1)
    - salt (.25tsp)

## Prep

1. Pick over and rinse lentils (.5c) in a fine-mesh colander
1. Peel garlic (1), leave it whole
1. Chop basil (opt), grate Parmesan (opt)
1. Set aside
    - marinara sauce (2c)
    - vegetable broth (2c)
    - bay leaf (1)
    - salt (.25tsp)

## Cook

1. Small saucepan: lentils (.5c), bay leaf (1), garlic (1), salt (.25tsp), broth
    (2c)
    1. Simmer over med-high, then reduce to a gentle simmer
    1. Cook until tender (20-35m), adding water if it runs dry
    1. Drain off excess liquid, discard the bay leaf and garlic
1. Large pot of salted water, cook pasta (8oz) al dente, drain, return it to the
    pot
1. Stir marinara (2c) into the lentils, warm through over medium heat
1. Bowls: pasta, then the lentil marinara
1. Garnish with Parmesan and basil (opt)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 35m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 25m
- Servings: 4
