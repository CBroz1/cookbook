# Coconut-Gochujang Glazed Chicken

!!! note
    Pasted by hand from NYT Cooking; the source URL was not recorded.

## Shopping

- Produce
    - ginger (2in, .33c)
    - broccoli florets (1lb)
    - scallions or cilantro
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - chicken breasts, boneless skinless (1.5lb)
    - coconut milk, unsweetened (.5c)
    - gochujang paste (2tbsp)
- Staples
    - canola oil (2tbsp)
    - turbinado sugar (3tbsp) or light brown sugar (2tbsp)
    - soy sauce, low-sodium (2tbsp)
    - kosher salt
    - black pepper
    - white rice

## Prep

1. Cut chicken (1.5lb) into 1.5in pieces, season with salt and pepper
1. Ginger (2in), peel and cut into matchsticks (.33c)
1. Broccoli (1lb), cut florets into 2in pieces
1. Slice scallions or chop cilantro for serving
1. Sauce bowl
    - coconut milk (.5c)
    - turbinado sugar (3tbsp)
    - gochujang paste (2tbsp)
    - soy sauce (2tbsp)

## Cook

1. Start the rice
1. Large nonstick skillet, med-high heat, canola oil (2tbsp)
    1. Add chicken, stir occasionally until golden all over (3m)
    1. Add ginger (.33c), stir until softened (2m)
    1. Add the sauce bowl, bring to a simmer, stirring until the gochujang
        dissolves
    1. Reduce to med-low, simmer until chicken is cooked through (5m)
1. Meanwhile, blanch broccoli (1lb) in salted boiling water until crisp-tender
    (2m), then drain
1. Plate chicken and broccoli, spoon the sauce over, serve with rice and the
    scallions or cilantro

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 35m
    - Prep: 15m
    - Cook: 20m
- Servings: 4
