# Creamy Pumpkin Marinara

## Shopping

- Produce
    - yellow onion (1)
    - red bell pepper (1)
    - garlic (2)
    - parsley (opt)
- Dairy
    - butter (1-2tbsp)
    - Parmesan (opt)
- Middle
    - diced or crushed tomatoes (15oz)
    - pumpkin puree (15oz)
- Staples
    - olive oil (2tbsp)
    - salt (.5tsp)
    - dried oregano (.5tsp)
    - dried tarragon (.25tsp)
    - ground cinnamon (.25tsp)
    - balsamic vinegar (1-2tsp)
    - pepper

## Prep

1. Chop onion (1) and red bell pepper (1)
1. Press garlic (2)
1. Chop parsley (opt), grate Parmesan (opt)
1. Spice bowl
    - dried oregano (.5tsp)
    - dried tarragon (.25tsp)
    - ground cinnamon (.25tsp)

## Cook

1. Large skillet, medium heat, olive oil (2tbsp) until shimmering
    1. Add onion (1), bell pepper (1), salt (.25tsp)
    1. Stir often until very tender (6-8m)
    1. Add garlic (2) and the spice bowl, stir until fragrant (1m)
    1. Add tomatoes (15oz), stir (1m)
    1. Add pumpkin puree (15oz), stir to combine, warm through
1. To a blender with butter (1tbsp) and balsamic vinegar (1tsp), blend until
    very smooth
1. Season with pepper and salt (.25tsp). More butter for richness, more vinegar
    for tang
1. Stir into warm pasta, top with Parmesan and parsley (opt)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 25m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 15m
- Yield: 4c
