# Steel-Cut Oat Risotto with Butternut Squash and Kale

## Shopping

- Produce
    - red onion (1 medium)
    - butternut squash (1 small, 2lb) or sweet potatoes (2)
    - garlic (4)
    - kale (2c packed, about .5 bunch)
    - lemon (1, 1tbsp juice)
- Dairy
    - Parmesan (.75c, 1.75oz)
    - butter (2tbsp)
- Middle
    - steel-cut oats (1.5c)
    - dry white wine (.5c)
- Staples
    - olive oil (2tbsp)
    - salt (1tsp)
    - red pepper flakes (pinch)
    - pepper

## Prep

1. Chop onion (1)
1. Peel and dice butternut squash (2lb) into .5in cubes
1. Press garlic (4)
1. Derib and chop kale (2c packed)
1. Grate Parmesan (.75c)
1. Juice lemon (1tbsp)
1. Measure water (6c) and white wine (.5c)

## Cook

1. Dutch oven or soup pot, olive oil (2tbsp) until shimmering
    1. Add onion (1), squash (2lb), salt (.5tsp), red pepper flakes
    1. Stir often until the onion is translucent and the squash is starting to
        soften (8-10m)
    1. Add garlic (4), stir to combine
    1. Add oats (1.5c) and kale (2c), stir until the kale wilts (2m)
    1. Add wine (.5c), scraping up any browned bits
    1. Add water (6c) and salt (.5tsp), raise to high and bring to a simmer
    1. Reduce to med-low, stir often, until tender and creamy (25-30m)
1. Off heat, stir in Parmesan (.75c), butter (2tbsp), lemon juice (1tbsp) and
    several twists of pepper
1. Rest (5m), then season generously and serve

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 60m
    - Prep: 20m
    - Cook: 40m
- Servings: 4-6
