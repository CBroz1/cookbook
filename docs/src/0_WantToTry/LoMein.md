# Lo Mein

## Shopping

- Produce
    - garlic (2)
    - ginger (.5tsp)
    - cremini mushrooms (2c)
    - broccoli florets (1c)
    - carrots (.5c)
    - red bell pepper (.5)
    - baby spinach or bok choy (3c)
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - lo mein noodles (6oz)
- Staples
    - soy sauce (2tbsp)
    - dark soy sauce (1tsp)
    - black vinegar or rice wine vinegar (.5tbsp)
    - sesame oil (1.5tbsp plus 1tsp)
    - honey (1.5tsp)
    - sesame seeds

## Prep

1. Slice mushrooms (2c), julienne carrots (.5c), slice bell pepper (.5)
1. Mince garlic (2), grate ginger (.5tsp)
1. Sauce bowl, whisk
    - soy sauce (2tbsp)
    - dark soy sauce (1tsp)
    - black vinegar (.5tbsp)
    - sesame oil (1tsp)
    - honey (1.5tsp)
    - ginger (.5tsp)

## Cook

1. Cook lo mein noodles (6oz) per the package, drain
1. Large skillet or wok, med-high heat, sesame oil (1.5tbsp)
    1. Add garlic (2), mushrooms (2c), broccoli (1c), carrots (.5c), bell pepper
        (.5)
    1. Stir fry until slightly tender (3m)
    1. Stir in spinach (3c), cook until wilted (2-3m)
    1. Stir in the noodles and the sauce, toss to combine
1. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 15m
    - Prep: 5m
    - Cook: 10m
- Servings: 4
