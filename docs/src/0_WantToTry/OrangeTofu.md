# Orange Tofu

!!! note
    Pasted by hand; the source URL was not recorded. Needs an air fryer.

## Shopping

- Produce
    - oranges (1-2)
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - extra-firm tofu (14-16oz)
- Staples
    - cornstarch (1-2tbsp)
    - honey (.25c)
    - soy sauce, tamari or liquid aminos (1tbsp)
    - toasted sesame oil (1tbsp)
    - cider vinegar (1tsp)
    - ground ginger (.25tsp)
    - crushed red pepper flakes (.25tsp)
    - fine salt (.5tsp)
    - white or black pepper (pinch)

## Prep

1. Drain tofu (14-16oz), cut into cubes
1. Zest oranges (1-2tsp) and juice them (6tbsp)
1. Marinade bowl, whisk
    - soy sauce (1tbsp)
    - toasted sesame oil (1tbsp)
    - fine salt (.5tsp)
    - white or black pepper (pinch)
1. Add tofu, stir to coat
1. Add cornstarch (1-2tbsp), stir to coat
1. Sauce bowl
    - orange zest (1-2tsp)
    - orange juice (6tbsp)
    - honey (.25c)
    - cider vinegar (1tsp)
    - ground ginger (.25tsp)
    - crushed red pepper flakes (.25tsp)

## Cook

1. Air fryer to 400
1. Tofu to the basket, air fry until crisp (11-13m), shaking halfway
1. Meanwhile reduce the sauce in the microwave
    1. Microwave (2m), whisk
    1. Microwave (1m), whisk
    1. Repeat until it looks like melted honey and has reduced to just under .5c
1. Drizzle half the sauce over the tofu, stir to coat

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 30m
    - Prep: 15m
    - Cook: 15m
- Servings: 4
