# Pearl Couscous Skillet

## Shopping

- Produce
    - shallot (1)
    - chives (.25c)
    - mint (3-4tbsp, opt)
    - kale (4-5 stalks)
- Dairy
    - feta (.5c, crumbled, opt)
- Middle
    - chickpeas (14oz)
    - diced tomatoes (14oz, fire-roasted)
    - pearl couscous (.5c)
    - vegetable broth (.75c)
- Staples
    - olive oil (5tbsp)
    - cumin (.5tsp)
    - salt (1tsp)
    - red wine vinegar (1tbsp)
    - maple syrup (1tbsp)
    - pepper

## Prep

1. Slice shallot (1) thin
1. Drain and rinse chickpeas (14oz)
1. Chop chives (.25c) and mint (3-4tbsp, opt)
1. Kale (4-5 stalks), destem and tear into bite-size pieces
1. Kale dressing bowl, whisk
    - olive oil (3tbsp)
    - red wine vinegar (1tbsp)
    - maple syrup (1tbsp)
    - salt and pepper
1. Set aside
    - diced tomatoes (14oz)
    - vegetable broth (.75c)
    - pearl couscous (.5c)
    - feta (.5c, opt)

## Cook

1. Skillet, medium heat, olive oil (2tbsp)
    1. Add shallot (1) and cumin (.5tsp), saute until softened (5m)
    1. Add chickpeas (14oz), mash gently a few times for texture
    1. Add tomatoes (14oz), bring to a simmer
    1. Add broth (.75c), salt (1tsp), couscous (.5c), simmer
    1. Cover, low heat, until couscous is soft and has absorbed most of the
        liquid (6-8m)
    1. Stir in chives (.25c)
1. Massage kale with its dressing until tender (1-2m)
1. Serve the couscous with the kale alongside, topped with mint (opt) and feta
    (opt)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 30m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 20m
- Servings: 2-3
