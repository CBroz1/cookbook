# Nutella Brownies

## Shopping

- Produce (none)
- Dairy
    - unsalted butter (.33c, 71g)
    - eggs (2)
- Middle
    - Nutella (1.25c, 370g)
    - semi-sweet chocolate chips (.75c, 135g, opt)
- Staples
    - light brown sugar (.5c, 100g)
    - vanilla extract (2tsp)
    - salt (.5tsp)
    - flour (.75c, 94g)
    - sea salt (opt)

## Prep

1. Butter (.33c) and eggs (2) to room temperature
1. Line 8in square pan with foil, overhang on all sides
1. Beat butter (.33c) on high until smooth and creamy (1m)
1. Add brown sugar (.5c), beat on high until creamed (1-2m)
1. On low, add eggs (2) and vanilla (2tsp); beat med-high to combine
1. Beat in Nutella (1c) and salt (.5tsp) until smooth and fluffy
1. On low, add flour (.75c), mix just until combined - do not overmix
1. Fold in chocolate chips (.75c, opt)

## Cook

1. Oven to 350
1. Pour batter into pan
1. Drop teaspoonfuls of remaining Nutella (.25c) on top, swirl with a knife
1. Sprinkle with sea salt (opt)
1. Bake until a toothpick shows a few moist crumbs (32-36m)
1. Cool completely in pan on a rack (3h)
1. Lift out by the foil, cut into squares

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 3h46m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 36m
    - Rest: 3h
- Servings: 12 brownies
