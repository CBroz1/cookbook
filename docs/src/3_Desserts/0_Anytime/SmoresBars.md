# S'mores Bars

## Shopping

- Produce (none)
- Dairy
    - unsalted butter (.5c)
    - egg (1)
- Middle
    - graham crackers (5 full-size, 2c chopped)
    - mini marshmallows (1c, heaping)
    - semi-sweet chocolate chips (1c)
- Staples
    - light brown sugar (1c, packed)
    - vanilla extract (1tbsp)
    - flour (.75c)
    - cooking spray

## Prep

1. Line 8x8 pan with foil, spray with cooking spray
1. Chop graham crackers (5, 2c) - keep the crumbs
1. Microwave-safe bowl: melt butter (.5c) on high (1m), let cool a moment so the
    egg does not scramble
1. Whisk in until smooth
    - egg (1)
    - light brown sugar (1c)
    - vanilla extract (1tbsp)
1. Stir in flour (.75c) just until combined - do not overmix
1. Fold in
    - graham crackers and crumbs (2c)
    - mini marshmallows (1c)
    - semi-sweet chocolate chips (1c)

## Cook

1. Oven to 350
1. Turn batter into pan, smooth the top
1. Bake until the center firms and the edges set (20-22m)
1. Cool in pan (30m) before slicing

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 58m
    - Prep: 8m
    - Cook: 20m
    - Rest: 30m
- Servings: 12 bars
