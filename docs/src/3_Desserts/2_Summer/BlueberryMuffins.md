# Blueberry Muffins

## Shopping

- Produce
    - Blueberries (1c)
- Dairy
    - Plain Yog (1c)
    - Eggs (2)
- Middle (none)
- Staples
    - Flour (1.8c)
    - Baking powder (1tsp)
    - Baking soda (.5tsp)
    - Salt (.5tsp)
    - Cinnamon (.25tsp, opt)
    - Oil (.3c)
    - Honey or maple syrup (.5c)
    - Vanilla extract (2tsp)
    - Sugar (1tbsp, for tops)
    - Butter

## Prep

!!! note
    Bring the eggs to room temperature.

1. Grease muffin tin w/butter
1. Beat 2 eggs
1. Dry 1:
    - Flour 1.75c
    - Baking powder (1tsp)
    - Baking soda (.5tsp)
    - Salt (.5tsp)
    - Cinnamon (.25tsp, opt)
1. Dry 2:
    - Blueberries
    - 1tsp flour
1. Wet 1:
    - Oil (.3c)
    - Honey or maple syrup (.5c)
1. Wet 2:
    - Plain Yog (1c)
    - Vanilla extract (2tsp)

## Cook

1. Oven to 400
1. Bowl of oil, honey/maple
    1. Beat in eggs
    1. Add yogurt/vanilla
    1. Mix well
1. Pour wet in dry
1. Fold in blueberries
1. Sprinkle tops with sugar (1tbsp)
1. Bake 16-19m

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 35m
    - Prep: 13m
    - Cook: 22m
- Servings: 12
