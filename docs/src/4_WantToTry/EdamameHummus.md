# Edamame Hummus

## Shopping

- Produce
    - lemon (1, 3tbsp juice plus .5tsp zest)
    - garlic (1)
    - parsley (1tbsp)
    - cucumber and celery, for serving
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - edamame, frozen shelled (.5lb, 1.5c)
    - tahini (.25c)
    - olives, for serving
- Staples
    - olive oil (3tbsp)
    - kosher salt (.75tsp)
    - ground cumin (.5tsp)
    - ground coriander (.25tsp)

## Prep

1. Zest lemon (.5tsp), then juice it (3tbsp)
1. Smash garlic (1)
1. Chop parsley (1tbsp)
1. Slice cucumber and celery for serving

## Cook

1. Boil edamame (1.5c) in salted water (4-5m), or microwave covered (2-3m)
1. Food processor
    1. Edamame, tahini (.25c), water (.25c)
    1. Lemon zest (.5tsp) and juice (3tbsp), garlic (1)
    1. Kosher salt (.75tsp), cumin (.5tsp), coriander (.25tsp)
    1. Puree until smooth
    1. Motor running, drizzle in olive oil (2tbsp) until absorbed
1. Into a bowl, stir in parsley (1tbsp), drizzle with olive oil (1tbsp)
1. Serve with cucumber, celery and olives

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 15m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 5m
- Yield: 1.25c
