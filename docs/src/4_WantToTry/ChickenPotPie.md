# Chicken Pot Pie

## Shopping

- Produce
    - onion (.33c)
- Dairy
    - refrigerated pie crusts (14.1oz box, 2)
    - butter (.33c)
    - milk (.5c)
- Middle
    - cooked chicken or turkey (2.5c, shredded)
    - chicken broth (1.75c)
    - frozen mixed vegetables (2c)
- Staples
    - flour (.33c)
    - salt (.5tsp)
    - pepper (.25tsp)

## Prep

1. Crusts out of the box to room temperature (15m)
1. Thaw mixed vegetables (2c)
1. Chop onion (.33c)
1. Shred cooked chicken (2.5c)
1. Line 9in pie plate with one crust, pressed to the side and bottom
1. Set aside
    - chicken broth (1.75c)
    - milk (.5c)
    - flour (.33c)
    - salt (.5tsp)
    - pepper (.25tsp)

## Cook

1. Oven to 425
1. Saucepan, medium heat, butter (.33c)
    1. Add onion (.33c), cook until tender (2m)
    1. Stir in flour (.33c), salt (.5tsp), pepper (.25tsp) until blended
    1. Gradually stir in broth (1.75c) and milk (.5c), until bubbly and thick
    1. Stir in chicken (2.5c) and vegetables (2c), off heat
1. Spoon filling into the lined pie plate
1. Top with the second crust, fold the edge under, seal and flute
1. Cut slits in the top crust
1. Bake (15m)
1. Cover the crust edge with foil, bake until golden (15-25m)
1. Rest (5m)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 65m
    - Prep: 25m
    - Cook: 40m
- Servings: 6
