# Dal Palak

## Shopping

- Produce
    - lime (1)
    - onion (1 small)
    - garlic (2)
    - ginger (1tsp)
    - spinach (3c)
    - cilantro
- Dairy
    - ghee or unsalted butter (2tbsp)
- Middle
    - masoor dal, red lentils (1c)
- Staples
    - ground turmeric (.25tsp)
    - kosher salt (1.5tsp)
    - black mustard seeds (.5tsp)
    - cumin seeds (.5tsp)
    - fennel seed (.5tsp)
    - dried red chile (1 small)
    - white rice

## Prep

1. Rinse masoor dal (1c) in a fine-mesh sieve
1. Chop onion (1) coarsely, garlic (2) fine
1. Grate ginger (1tsp)
1. Chop spinach (3c) coarsely
1. Halve the lime (1)
1. Seed bowl
    - black mustard seeds (.5tsp)
    - cumin seeds (.5tsp)
    - fennel seed (.5tsp)

## Cook

1. Start the rice
1. Saucepan: dal (1c) and water (3c), boil over med-high heat
    1. Skim the foam until it stops foaming (3m)
    1. Stir in turmeric (.25tsp)
    1. Reduce to med-low, partially cover, simmer until tender (25-30m)
    1. Off heat, squeeze in juice from half the lime, stir in salt (1tsp)
1. High-sided skillet, med-high heat, ghee (2tbsp)
    1. Add the seed bowl, cook until mustard seeds pop (seconds)
    1. Reduce to medium, add chile (1), stir to coat
    1. Add onion (1), stir until translucent and softened (5m)
    1. Add garlic (2) and ginger (1tsp), until fragrant (30s)
    1. Add spinach (3c) and salt (.5tsp), until wilted but still bright green
        (1m)
    1. Off heat, squeeze in juice from the remaining lime half
1. Stir the spinach mixture into the dal, salt to taste
1. Serve over rice, topped with cilantro and lime wedges

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 45m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 35m
- Servings: 4
