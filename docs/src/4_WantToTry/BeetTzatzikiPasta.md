# Beet Pasta

## Shopping

- Produce
    - baby arugula (1c)
    - lemons (2, .5c juice plus 1tbsp zest)
- Dairy
    - goat cheese (.5c, crumbled)
- Middle
    - beet fusilli (1lb)
    - pine nuts (.33c)
- Staples
    - olive oil (.25c)
    - salt and pepper

## Prep

1. Zest a lemon (1tbsp), then juice lemons (.5c)
1. Crumble goat cheese (.5c)
1. Toast pine nuts (.33c)
1. Sauce bowl, whisk
    - olive oil (.25c)
    - lemon juice (.5c)

## Cook

1. Large pot of salted water, cook fusilli (1lb) al dente
1. Drain, reserving pasta water (1c)
1. Toss pasta with the lemon-oil sauce
1. Loosen with pasta water, .25c at a time, if it looks dry
1. Gently stir in arugula (1c), lemon zest (1tbsp), goat cheese (.5c)
1. Salt and pepper, garnish with pine nuts (.33c)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 13m
    - Prep: 5m
    - Cook: 8m
- Servings: 6
