# Herbed Quinoa Chickpea Salad

## Shopping

- Produce
    - baby spinach (1.5c)
    - parsley (.5c)
    - cilantro (.5c)
    - green onion (.33c)
    - garlic (1)
    - lemons (1-2, 3-4tbsp juice)
- Dairy
    - feta (.33c, crumbled)
- Middle
    - chickpeas (15oz)
    - tahini (2tbsp)
    - pepitas (.33c)
- Staples
    - quinoa (1c)
    - olive oil (.25c)
    - fine sea salt (.5tsp)
    - pepper

## Prep

1. Rinse quinoa (1c) in a fine-mesh colander
1. Drain and rinse chickpeas (15oz)
1. Chop
    - baby spinach (1.5c)
    - parsley (.5c)
    - cilantro (.5c)
    - green onion (.33c), green parts only
1. Crumble feta (.33c)
1. Dressing bowl, whisk
    - olive oil (.25c)
    - lemon juice (3tbsp, up to 4 to taste)
    - tahini (2tbsp)
    - garlic (1), pressed
    - fine sea salt (.5tsp)
    - pepper

## Cook

1. Saucepan: quinoa (1c) and water (2c), boil over med-high heat
    1. Reduce to a gentle simmer until the water is absorbed (15m)
    1. Off heat, cover, steam (5m), then fluff
1. Small skillet, medium heat, toast pepitas (.33c) until fragrant and popping
    (5m), then into a large serving bowl to cool
1. Add to the bowl
    - quinoa
    - chickpeas (15oz)
    - spinach (1.5c)
    - parsley (.5c)
    - cilantro (.5c)
    - green onion (.33c)
    - feta (.33c)
1. Drizzle with the dressing and toss

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 35m
    - Prep: 15m
    - Cook: 20m
- Servings: 4 side servings
