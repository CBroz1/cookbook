# Veggie Meatballs with Tahini Sauce

## Shopping

- Produce
    - cauliflower florets (2c, about .5 head)
    - garlic (4)
    - cilantro (1tbsp)
    - lemon (1, .25c juice)
    - parsley (1.5tbsp)
- Dairy
    - eggs (2)
- Middle
    - cooked quinoa (3c)
    - chickpeas (1c)
    - breadcrumbs (1c)
    - tahini paste (.5c)
- Staples
    - onion powder (1tsp)
    - italian seasoning (.5tbsp)
    - paprika (.5tbsp)
    - salt (1.5tsp)
    - pepper (.5tsp)
    - olive oil (3tbsp)
    - ground cumin (.125tsp)

## Prep

1. Cook quinoa (3c) ahead, or use leftovers
1. Drain and rinse chickpeas (1c)
1. Chop cilantro (1tbsp) and parsley (1.5tbsp)
1. Juice lemon (.25c)
1. Line a baking tray
1. Meatball spices
    - onion powder (1tsp)
    - italian seasoning (.5tbsp)
    - paprika (.5tbsp)
    - salt (1tsp)
    - pepper (.5tsp)

## Cook

1. Oven to 375
1. Boil cauliflower (2c) until tender (5m), drain, into a food processor
1. Add and puree on high until combined
    - cooked quinoa (3c)
    - chickpeas (1c)
    - breadcrumbs (1c)
    - garlic (2)
    - cilantro (1tbsp)
    - the meatball spices
1. Into a large bowl, mix in eggs (2) until fully incorporated
1. Let the mixture sit (5m), roll into 1in balls onto the tray
1. Bake until golden and firm outside, soft inside (30-35m)
1. Meanwhile blend the tahini sauce
    - tahini paste (.5c)
    - lemon juice (.25c)
    - lukewarm water (.33c)
    - olive oil (3tbsp)
    - garlic (2)
    - ground cumin (.125tsp)
    - salt (.5tsp)
1. Stir parsley (1.5tbsp) into the sauce, serve alongside

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 40m
    - Prep: 10m
    - Cook: 30m
- Servings: 40 meatballs
