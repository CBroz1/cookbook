# Miso Maple Tofu

## Shopping

- Produce
    - ginger (1in)
    - scallions (2)
    - garlic (1)
    - carrots (2)
    - cilantro
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - firm tofu (14oz)
    - white miso (1tbsp)
    - edamame, shelled (1c, cooked)
- Staples
    - soy sauce or coconut aminos (.25c plus 2tbsp)
    - maple syrup (2tbsp)
    - toasted sesame oil (2tsp)
    - cornstarch (5tbsp)
    - rice vinegar (1tbsp)
    - crushed red pepper flakes (.5tsp)
    - white rice
    - toasted sesame seeds (1tbsp)

## Prep

1. Wrap tofu (14oz) in a towel on a sheet, weigh it down, let sit (10m)
1. Cut tofu into 1in cubes
1. Grate ginger (1in) and garlic (1)
1. Slice scallions (2) thin
1. Carrots (2), peel and cut into matchsticks
1. Marinade bowl, whisk
    - ginger (1in), grated
    - soy sauce (.25c)
    - maple syrup (1tbsp)
    - toasted sesame oil (1tsp)
1. Add tofu, toss gently, sprinkle cornstarch (5tbsp), mix, let sit (10m)
1. Dressing bowl, whisk
    - scallions (2)
    - garlic (1), grated
    - white miso (1tbsp)
    - rice vinegar (1tbsp)
    - crushed red pepper flakes (.5tsp)
    - soy sauce (2tbsp)
    - maple syrup (1tbsp)
    - toasted sesame oil (1tsp)

## Cook

1. Oven to 400
1. Start the rice
1. Lift tofu out of the marinade onto a parchment-lined sheet
1. Bake, turning halfway, until golden with a thin crust (25-30m)
1. Bowls: rice, then carrots (2), edamame (1c), tofu
1. Drizzle with the dressing, sprinkle sesame seeds (1tbsp), top with cilantro

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 65m
    - Prep: 15m
    - Cook: 30m
    - Rest: 20m
- Servings: 4
