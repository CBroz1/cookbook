# Vegan Stuffed Peppers

## Shopping

- Produce
    - bell peppers (6 medium)
    - onions (2 small)
    - crimini mushrooms (1c)
    - carrots (2 small)
    - zucchini (1 small)
    - parsley (.75c)
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - brown rice (.75c, uncooked)
    - tomato puree (.75c)
- Staples
    - olive oil (2-3tbsp)
    - salt
    - pepper
    - chili powder (pinch)

## Prep

1. Cut the tops off the bell peppers (6) and keep them as lids
1. Scoop out the insides, discard the seeds
1. Dice onions (2), slice mushrooms (1c) and carrots (2), cube zucchini (1)
1. Chop parsley (.75c) fine
1. Lightly grease a baking pan, stand the peppers in it

## Cook

1. Oven to 425
1. Cook brown rice (.75c) in salted water per the package, drain
1. Large skillet, olive oil (2-3tbsp) until sizzling hot (1m)
    1. Add onions (2), saute until soft (2-3m)
    1. Add mushrooms (1c), cook until soft (5-8m)
    1. Add carrots (2) and zucchini (1), cook until tender
    1. Add the rice, parsley (.33c) and tomato puree (.75c), stir
    1. Season with salt, pepper and chili powder (pinch)
1. Fill the peppers with the rice mixture, set the lids on top
1. Bake until the peppers soften and brown at the edges (20-25m)
1. Sprinkle with the remaining parsley and serve warm

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 50m
    - Prep: 15m
    - Cook: 35m
- Servings: 6
