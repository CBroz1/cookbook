# Vegan Nacho Cheese Sauce

## Shopping

- Produce
    - lemon (1 large, 3tbsp juice)
- Dairy (none)
- Middle
    - raw cashews (2c)
- Staples
    - nutritional yeast (.5c)
    - smoked paprika (.5tsp)
    - garlic powder (1tsp)
    - onion powder (1tsp)
    - chili powder (.5tsp)
    - salt (2tsp)
    - Sriracha (1-2tsp, opt)
    - turmeric (.5tsp, opt)

## Prep

!!! note
    Quick-soak the cashews in boiling water, or soak them cold for several hours or
    overnight.

1. Soak cashews (2c), then drain
1. Juice lemon (3tbsp)
1. Spice bowl
    - nutritional yeast (.5c)
    - smoked paprika (.5tsp)
    - garlic powder (1tsp)
    - onion powder (1tsp)
    - chili powder (.5tsp)
    - salt (2tsp)
    - turmeric (.5tsp, opt, for colour)

## Cook

1. High-powered blender
    1. Cashews (2c), lemon juice (3tbsp), water (3c)
    1. The spice bowl
    1. Blend until completely smooth
1. Medium saucepan, medium heat
    1. Whisk constantly until it bubbles and thickens
    1. Add water (1c), or less, to the consistency you want
1. Salt to taste, Sriracha (1-2tsp, opt)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 25m
    - Prep: 20m
    - Cook: 5m
- Servings: 12
- Substitutions: thickens on standing; rewarm with a splash of water
