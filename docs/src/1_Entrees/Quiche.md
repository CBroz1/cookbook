# Quiche

## Shopping

- Produce (none)
- Dairy
    - eggs (4)
    - whole milk (.5c)
    - heavy cream (.5c)
    - shredded cheese (1c)
    - greens side for quiche
- Middle
    - quiche mixins, ham, mushrooms (2c, opt)
    - frozen pie crust (1)
- Staples
    - salt/pepper (.25tsp)

## Prep

1. Thaw frozen pie crust
1. Grease 9in pie dish
1. Grate cheese (1c)
1. Chop mixins (2c, opt): ham, mushrooms
1. Set aside
    - eggs (4)
    - whole milk (.5c)
    - heavy cream (.5c)
    - salt/pepper (.25tsp)

## Cook

1. Oven to 375
1. Blind bake crust
    1. Add pie weights or dried beans
    1. Bake until edges start to brown (15-16m)
    1. Remove weights, prick bottom all over with a fork
    1. Return to oven (7-8m)
    1. Let cool
1. Oven to 350
1. Whisk filling
    - eggs (4)
    - whole milk (.5c)
    - heavy cream (.5c)
    - shredded cheese (1c)
    - mixins (2c, opt)
    - salt/pepper (.25tsp)
1. Pour filling into cooled crust
1. Bake until center just set (45-55m)
1. Cool (15m)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 4h00m
    - Prep: 2h40m
    - Cook: 1h20m
- Servings: 4
