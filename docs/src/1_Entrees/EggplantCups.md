# Eggplant Cups

## Shopping

- Produce
    - eggplant (2)
    - fresh basil (.25c)
    - fresh oregano (2tbsp)
- Dairy
    - eggs (2)
    - fresh ricotta (400g)
    - parmesan (.33c)
    - mozzarella (1c)
- Middle
    - tomato pasta sauce (1c)
    - pine nuts (.33c, opt)
- Staples
    - oil spray

## Prep

!!! note
    Source cuts the eggplant into 6 slices; I go thinner.

1. Trim eggplant, slice thin
1. Bowl
    1. Grated Parmesan (.33c)
    1. Beaten eggs (2)
    1. Fresh ricotta (400g)
    1. Chopped basil (.25c)
    1. Chopped oregano (2tbsp)
1. Grate Mozzarella (1c)
1. Set out
    - tomato pasta sauce (1c)
    - pine nuts (.33c)
1. Spray muffin pan

## Cook

1. Oven to 410
1. Eggplant
    1. Spray both sides with oil
    1. Fry in batches (3-4m each side)
    1. Set aside to cool (5m)
1. Muffin pan
    1. Cut each eggplant slice in half
    1. Line pan with slices
    1. Scoop ricotta mixture into each cup
    1. Add pasta sauce, mozzarella, pine nuts
1. Bake (20-25m)
1. Let rest (10m)

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 80m
    - Prep: 35m
    - Cook: 45m
- Servings: 6 (makes 12)
