# Kolokithokeftedes (Zucchini Fritters)

!!! note
    Source buys the tzatziki. Easy enough to make with yogurt, cucumber, garlic and
    lemon.

## Shopping

- Produce
    - zucchini (6, 3c grated)
    - scallions (8)
    - dill (6tbsp)
    - garlic (1)
    - cucumber (1)
    - lemon (1)
- Dairy
    - feta (1.5c)
    - eggs (4)
    - greek yogurt (2c)
- Middle
- Staples
    - kosher salt (1.5tsp)
    - nutmeg (.5tsp)
    - flour (1c)
    - olive oil (4tbsp)
    - salt and pepper

## Prep

1. Grate zucchini (3c)
    1. Spread out, sprinkle kosher salt (1.5tsp)
    1. Let sit (30m)
    1. Wrap in a towel, squeeze out moisture
1. Tzatziki
    1. Cucumber (1), grate (.5c), squeeze out moisture
    1. Greek yogurt (2c)
    1. Garlic (1), press
    1. Lemon juice (1tsp)
1. Bowl
    1. Zucchini
    1. Dill (6tbsp), chop
    1. Scallions (8), chop
    1. Nutmeg (.5tsp)
    1. Feta (1.5c), crumble
    1. Eggs (4), beat
    1. Flour (1c), more if too wet to shape
    1. Salt and pepper
1. Form into 3-4in patties

## Cook

1. Large skillet, med-high heat, olive oil (4tbsp) until shimmering
    1. Add patties in batches, 1in apart
    1. Fry until golden (3m)
    1. Flip, fry until golden (2m)
1. Serve with tzatziki

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 58m
    - Prep: 18m
    - Cook: 10m
    - Rest: 30m
- Servings: 6 (13 fritters)
