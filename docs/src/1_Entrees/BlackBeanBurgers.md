# Black Bean Burgers

## Shopping

- Produce
    - Onion (2)
    - Mushrooms
    - Garlic cloves (2)
    - Cilantro (.25c)
- Dairy
    - Eggs (2)
    - Pepper jack cheese slices
- Middle
    - Black beans (30oz)
    - Hamburger buns
- Staples
    - Pickles
    - Ketchup (2tbsp)
    - Worcestershire sauce (2tsp)
    - Hot sauce (.5tsp)
    - BBQ sauce (2tbsp)
    - Salt (.75tsp)
    - Black pepper (.5tsp)
    - Cumin (1tsp)
    - Chili powder (1tsp)
    - Smoked paprika (1tsp)
    - Breadcrumbs (.5c)
    - Olive oil (1tbsp)

## Prep

1. Drain and rinse black beans.
1. Caramelize 1 onion
1. Prep pan fry mushrooms
1. Aromatics
    - Finely chop 1 onion
    - Press 2 garlic cloves
1. Bowl, mix
    - 2 eggs
    - 2tbsp ketchup
    - 2tsp Worcestershire sauce
    - .5tsp hot sauce
    - 2tbsp BBQ sauce
    - .75tsp salt
    - .5tsp black pepper
    - 1tsp cumin
    - 1tsp chili powder
    - 1tsp smoked paprika
    - .5c breadcrumbs

## Cook

1. Preheat oven to 325
1. Spread beans on sheet, bake 15m to dry
1. For onion, garlic
    1. Pan fry until softened, 5-6m.
    1. Blot dry
    1. Add to bowl
1. Form the into 6 patties.
1. Oven to 400
1. Bake for 10m on each side.
1. Add Cheese, continue bake
1. Serve

## Notes

- Suggested Time: 45m
    - Prep: 30m
    - Cook: 15m
- Servings: 6
