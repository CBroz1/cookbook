    Mediterranean Couscous Salad¶

    Shopping¶

    • Produce
      • shallot (1 large, .5c)
      • garlic (2)
      • lemons (1-2, 2-4tbsp juice)
      • grape or cherry tomatoes (1 pint)
      • zucchini (1 medium)
      • yellow squash (1 small)
      • basil or parsley (.33c)
    • Dairy
      • feta (4oz, 1c crumbled)
    • Middle
      • Israeli couscous (1.33c)
      • pine nuts (.33c)
      • chickpeas (15oz)
      • Kalamata olives (.33c)
    • Staples
      • olive oil (.33c)
      • fine sea salt (.5tsp)
      • pepper

    Prep¶

    1. Drain and rinse chickpeas (15oz)
    2. Quarter tomatoes (1 pint)
    3. Slice zucchini (1) and yellow squash (1) into very thin rounds
    4. Chop shallot (.5c) fine, press garlic (2)
    5. Slice olives (.33c) thin, crumble feta (1c)
    6. Chop basil or parsley (.33c)
    7. Dressing, in a large serving bowl, whisk
      • olive oil (.33c)
      • lemon juice (2tbsp, up to 4 to taste)
      • shallot (.5c)
      • garlic (2)
      • fine sea salt (.5tsp)
      • pepper

    Cook¶

    1. Cook couscous (1.33c) al dente per the package, drain off excess water
    2. Large skillet, med-low heat, toast pine nuts (.33c) until lightly golden and fragrant - they burn fast - then into a bowl to cool
    3. Add couscous to the dressing bowl, toss to coat
    4. Top with and stir through
      • pine nuts (.33c)
      • chickpeas (15oz)
      • tomatoes (1 pint)
      • feta (1c)
      • olives (.33c)
      • zucchini and yellow squash
      • basil or parsley (.33c)
    5. Salt, pepper and more lemon juice to taste
    6. Best after chilling (30m)

    Notes¶

    • Suggested Time: 35m
      • Prep: 20m
      • Cook: 15m
    • Servings: 6
